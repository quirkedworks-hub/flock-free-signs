# FLOCK YOU!!! Free Printable Anti-ALPR Sign

A free, printable protest sign for the fight against Flock and other license-plate-reader
cameras. Made for city council meetings, public comment, candidate forums, and your front
window.

![FLOCK YOU!!! ...and the 4th Amendment](preview-flock-4th-amendment.jpg)

## Download

**[flock-free-sampler.zip](flock-free-sampler.zip)** has everything below in one file.
No email, no signup.

- `flock-4th-amendment-letter-landscape.pdf`: one print-ready design on US Letter,
  landscape. Any home printer or copy shop handles it. Tape it to foam board and it's a
  rally sign.
- Screen-quality previews of three other designs (*...and your privacy / ...and the
  Constitution / ...and the horse you rode in on*). These are previews, not print files.
- A QR image, printing notes, and the license.

## The one rule

Print as many as you want: for yourself, your street, your protest, your public comment.
**Don't sell prints or files.** Full license in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).

## How it was made

The art started as an AI draft. It was then vectorized, cleaned up by hand, and
print-prepped.
